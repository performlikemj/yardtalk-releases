# YardTalk Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

YardTalk is a Mac app made by By Way of MJ LLC ("we"). This policy covers the Mac App Store version and the direct-download version.

## The short version

- Your audio, screen recordings, camera video, and transcripts stay on your Mac.
- There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
- No account is needed.
- Data leaves your Mac only when you turn on one of the optional features below.

## What stays on your Mac

YardTalk records your microphone, your screen (only while a clip is recording), and your camera (only if you turn on the camera bubble). Speech is transcribed on your Mac using Apple's Neural Engine. Recordings, transcripts, notes, and reports are stored in YardTalk's folder on your Mac. You can delete them in the app at any time.

The first time you use transcription, YardTalk downloads its speech-recognition model files. That download contains no personal data.

## Optional features that send data

**Claude summaries (off by default).** If you choose Claude as the summary provider and enter your own Anthropic API key, YardTalk sends that session's transcripts, voice-note text, and notes to Anthropic's API to write the summary. Audio and video are never sent. Anthropic handles that data under your own Anthropic account and its privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy. The default provider is Apple's on-device model, which sends nothing. A local Ollama model also sends nothing off your Mac.

**NeighborhoodUnited upload (off by default).** If you link a NeighborhoodUnited account with a personal access token, YardTalk can upload a session summary there, but only after you approve each upload. The upload contains the project name and type, session start and end times, the summary, accomplishments, blockers, and next steps. Recordings and transcripts are never uploaded. NeighborhoodUnited is also operated by By Way of MJ LLC.

**License check (direct-download version only).** The direct-download version checks your license key with our server. The Mac App Store version does not; Apple handles the purchase.

## Where your keys are kept

Your Anthropic API key, NeighborhoodUnited token, and (direct-download version) license key are stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac.

## Children

YardTalk is not directed at children under 13.
