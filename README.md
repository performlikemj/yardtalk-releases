# YardTalk

**A narrated work journal for macOS.**

YardTalk lives in your menu bar and captures your work sessions the way you'd explain them to a colleague: short narrated screen clips, voice notes, and dictation — transcribed **entirely on-device** (Apple Neural Engine, no audio ever leaves your Mac) and synthesized into per-project reports when you wrap up.

- 🎙 **Dictation anywhere** — global hotkey, on-device streaming transcription, inserts at your cursor
- 📓 **Voice notes & narrated clips** — capture context while you work, review it as a timeline
- 🧠 **End-of-session synthesis** — your narration becomes structured notes: accomplishments, blockers, next steps
- 🔒 **Local-first** — clips, transcripts, and audio stay on your Mac; only summaries you approve are shared
- 🔁 **Auto-updates** via Sparkle

**Requirements:** macOS 14.2+ (Apple Silicon), a microphone, and Screen Recording permission for clips.
